Check the avalanche danger in Norway and Sweden at a glance, right on your Mac desktop.

The app shows the daily avalanche forecast from the Norwegian Avalanche Warning Service (varsom.no) and the Swedish Environmental Protection Agency (lavinprognoser.se) for every forecast region, or for the region you’re in.

• Desktop and Notification Center widgets with today’s or tomorrow’s danger level
• A map of all regions, colored by danger level
• Full forecasts with avalanche problems, exposed aspects and elevations
• Earlier and coming days, emergency warnings, and saved forecasts when you’re offline
• Ask Siri about your favorites or any region, including earlier days, and use forecasts in Shortcuts
• Machine translation of forecast text into your language
• Also on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, with full VoiceOver support

Always read the full forecast and make your own assessment in avalanche terrain.
