Skredvarsel 6.0 starts our sixth winter season: 2026/27.

New
- Ask Siri about your favorite regions, tomorrow’s forecast or an earlier day, such as yesterday or last Saturday. Shortcuts can get the forecast for any date, including past seasons.
- Siri answers in the language you ask in, and gives a spoken summary when you can’t see the screen.
- Made for iPhone Duo: your place, the map and the open forecast stay put when you fold or unfold, and wide screens show the forecast in two columns.
- A new tall widget with today’s forecast and the coming days (iOS 27).
- Choose the current or tomorrow’s forecast in widgets, and add a control that opens a forecast from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.
- Apple Watch suggests forecasts for your favorite regions in the morning, and its widgets let you choose a region and forecast day.

Improved
- Widgets refresh when new forecasts are expected, show clearer dates and download times, and tell you when no valid forecast is available.
- VoiceOver reads forecast text and active emergency warnings in widgets.
- More reliable Swedish evening updates, saved forecasts when a download fails, and the right forecast day when you travel.
- Smoother switching between regions, location updates, favorites sync, translation and sharing.
- Clearer forecast cards and better readability with large text. The app asks for your location only when you choose a location-based action.
